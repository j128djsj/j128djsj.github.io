About

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Beijing Jiaotong University. I work on agents for electronic design automation, reasoning in large language models, and speech and audio processing.

In 2027, I will join the School of Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) as a Ph.D. student in Computer Science, advised by Prof. Lei He (IEEE Fellow).
