About

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Beijing Jiaotong University.

My current research interests include agents for electronic design automation (EDA) and reasoning in large language models. My previous work focused on speech and audio processing.

In 2027, I will join the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) as a Ph.D. student in Computer Science, advised by Prof. Lei He (IEEE Fellow).
